# Project design brief

## Product
John Rodriguez Portfolio

## Type
Personal portfolio

## Audience
Hiring managers, founders, operators, and collaborators

## Goal
Show capability fast, establish credibility, and drive contact

## First viewport
- John Rodriguez is the strongest signal
- One sharp positioning statement
- One clear CTA
- One dominant visual field
- No clutter competing with identity

## Narrative sequence
- Identity
- Proof
- How John builds and thinks
- Selected work
- Contact CTA

## Visual direction
- Dark, premium, technical
- Strong hierarchy
- Memorable without clutter
- Polished but not overdesigned
- Motion should support presence, not distract

## Avoid
- Generic personal-brand hero patterns
- Feature-card overload
- Badge confetti
- Fake stats
- Too many sections with equal emphasis
